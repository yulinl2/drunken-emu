# Monitor deadline

**Sources:** [S6](src.s6.md)

A watch has a deadline: 5 minutes by default, at most 30 minutes, and at most 10 minutes under -p. At the deadline the watch ends and Claude gets one notice. Cap question: [Q6 (doubt): Which maximum applies to Monitor deadlines?](question.q6.md).
