# Enforce a policy with a mod of your own

**Sources:** [S58](src.s58.md)

To keep every user's mod out, you don't need a mod of your own. Set `allowManagedModsOnly`. Write a policy mod when you want to allow some users' mods and refuse others, or to record what mods do.
