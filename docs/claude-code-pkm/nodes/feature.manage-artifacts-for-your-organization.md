# Manage artifacts for your organization

**Sources:** [S33](src.s33.md)

Owners on Team and Enterprise plans control artifacts from claude.ai admin settings. Artifact content is stored on Anthropic-operated infrastructure and is visible only to authenticated members of the publishing organization and the people they share it with, unless the artifact is shared publicly.
